## 5. UI at broadcast scale

A desktop UI drawn at native web size (body 14-16 px) is unreadable when the whole window is shown on a 1920x1080 frame. Rule: **scale factor = 18 / native font px**, and crop or zoom the UI until every text is at least 18 px on screen.

Primitives in `src/primitives` draw their smallest labels at 15 px native: wrap them in `style={{ zoom: 1.2 to 1.7 }}` (min 1.200). Never reduce type instead of cropping.

| native font px | scale needed | native crop width for 16:9 | native crop width for 9:16 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 11 | 1.636 | 1173 | 660 |
| 12 | 1.500 | 1280 | 720 |
| 13 | 1.385 | 1386 | 780 |
| 14 | 1.286 | 1493 | 839 |
| 16 | 1.125 | 1706 | 960 |
| 18 | 1.000 | 1920 | 1080 |

Crop a native window of width W to the full frame: scale = frame width / W. Typical crops:

| native crop W | scale to 1920 (16:9) | 14 px body becomes | 12 px label becomes | ok? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1920 | 1.00 | 14.0 px | 12.0 px | no |
| 1600 | 1.20 | 16.8 px | 14.4 px | no |
| 1440 | 1.33 | 18.7 px | 16.0 px | body only |
| 1280 | 1.50 | 21.0 px | 18.0 px | yes |
| 1100 | 1.75 | 24.4 px | 20.9 px | yes |
| 960 | 2.00 | 28.0 px | 24.0 px | yes |

In 9:16 (1080 wide) a 390 px mobile screen scales x2.77 (14 px becomes 38.8 px); a 1440 px desktop crop needs a 760 px native crop (x1.42) to keep 14 px text at 20 px.

Practical recipe: build the UI at a native 960-1280 px wide, give the wrapper `zoom`, show 3-5 rows or cards (not 12), and let the camera push in on the focal control. Show one idea per beat.
