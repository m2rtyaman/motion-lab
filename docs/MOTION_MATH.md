## 2. Easing = velocity profile

An ease maps time fraction to progress; its *slope* is speed. `.out` = fast start, decelerate to rest (things arriving). `.in` = slow start, accelerate (things leaving). `.inOut` = accelerate then decelerate (A to B on screen). `linear` = constant speed, robotic because nothing in the physical world starts and stops instantly.

Rules: entrances `.out`; exits `.in`; moves between two on-screen positions `.inOut`. An `.in` entrance hits peak speed at the very end and stops dead (reads as a slam). An `.inOut` entrance wastes its first frames (power2.inOut is only 0.06 at 25% of the time). `back.out(1.6)` overshoots by 9.0% of the travel distance.

Raw `linear` and `easeInOut`-style names are lint errors (A002).

## 3. Springs

Model: `m x'' + c x' + k x = 0`. Natural frequency `w0 = sqrt(k/m)`; damping ratio `zeta = c / (2 sqrt(k m))`; for `zeta < 1` overshoot = `exp(-zeta pi / sqrt(1 - zeta^2))`; `zeta >= 1` never overshoots (1 = fastest no-bounce settle). Decay rate of the envelope = `zeta w0 = c / 2m`: bigger = settles sooner. Settle frames come from `getSpringSettleFrames` (threshold 0.001 on position AND velocity).

| SPRING preset | k / c / m | w0 (rad/s) | zeta | overshoot (formula) | overshoot (solver) | 1st peak (f) | settle (f) | bounce | use for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `snappy` | 240/28/0.8 | 17.3 | 1.01 | 0.0% | 0.0% | - | 44 | none | UI snaps, toggles, buttons |
| `rigid` | 300/35/1 | 17.3 | 1.01 | 0.0% | 0.0% | - | 44 | none | heavy panels, machinery, no life |
| `bouncy` | 180/14/0.9 | 14.1 | 0.55 | 12.6% | 12.6% | 16 | 63 | visible | playful pops, badges, stickers |
| `gentle` | 120/18/1 | 11.0 | 0.82 | 1.1% | 1.1% | 30 | 59 | slight | soft cards, slight life |
| `smooth` | 120/24/1 | 11.0 | 1.10 | 0.0% | 0.0% | - | 80 | none | large surfaces, calm follow |
| `liquidLead` | 260/22/0.8 | 18.0 | 0.76 | 2.5% | 2.5% | 16 | 33 | slight | leader of a liquid pair |
| `liquidTrail` | 160/26/1 | 12.6 | 1.03 | 0.0% | 0.0% | - | 62 | none | follower of liquidLead |
| `cameraTrack` | 160/22/1 | 12.6 | 0.87 | 0.4% | 0.4% | 30 | 54 | slight | camera/rig follow |
| `morphSnappy` | 210/22/0.85 | 15.7 | 0.82 | 1.0% | 1.0% | 21 | 41 | slight | container morphs |
| `snapback` | 260/18/0.9 | 17.0 | 0.59 | 10.2% | 10.2% | 14 | 54 | visible | elastic return after a drag |

Formula and solver agree for every preset (max difference under 0.2 points).

Pick: want no bounce -> `zeta >= 1` (`snappy`, `rigid`, `smooth`, `cameraTrack`...). Want life -> `zeta` 0.5-0.85. Heavier look -> raise mass (lower w0), not damping. A spring with long settle frames cannot finish inside a short beat; compare settle frames with the beat length. Use `createSpringEase(SPRING.x)` with `duration = getSpringSettleDuration(SPRING.x)` to run one inside GSAP.

In this repo: scenes use presets; spring tokens feed `createSpringEase`, the choreography helpers and `SPRING_PRESETS` (`snappy, rigid, bouncy, gentle`). Lint A006: damping must be >= 10.

| `normal` | 0.055 | 3.3 | 88% | 0.56 s (33.6 f) | 0.67 s (40.2 f) | 0.83 s (50.1 f) | 86% | 0.49 s (29.4 f) | 0.6 s (36 f) | 0.77 s (45.9 f) |
| `loose` | 0.08 | 4.8 | 82% | 0.61 s (36.6 f) | 0.77 s (46.2 f) | 1.01 s (60.6 f) | 79% | 0.54 s (32.4 f) | 0.7 s (42 f) | 0.94 s (56.4 f) |

Other tokens: `wordStagger` 0.06 s (3.6 f), `layerStagger` 0.07, `streakStagger` 0.04, `itemLead` 0.14 s (8.4 f lead before the first item), `crossCutOverlap` 0.16 s (9.6 f: incoming starts that long before the outgoing finishes).

Overlap rule: inside a group the repo overlaps ~85-95% (a wave, not a queue). Between *different* elements start the next when the previous is 50-70% done (30-50% overlap): earlier feels like a collision, later like a stutter. Follow-through: the child or accent finishes after the parent (pop settles after the overshoot, shake decays to rest, headline settles then `punch` lands).

In this repo: `enter(..,'stagger'|'words', at, { stagger })`; pick `tight` for characters/long lists, `normal` for cards, `loose` for 3-4 big items.

## 5. Anticipation & overshoot amounts

Anticipation = a small move the opposite way before the main move; overshoot = passing the target, then returning (follow-through). Repo magnitudes:

| what | amount | time | frames |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `pop` (scale 0 to 1) | to 1.06 (+6%), then back to 1 | 0.18 s in + 0.42 s settle | 10.8 + 25.2 = 36 |
| `punch` soft/normal/strong | scale 1.02 / 1.04 / 1.07 + brightness flash | 0.12 s up, 0.2 s down | 7.2 + 12 |
| `words` drop | y -42px, scale 0.88, rotateX 20, blur 8px; back.out overshoot 9.0% | 0.38 s | 22.8 |
| `shake` | 5 steps, amplitude 12px (x0.5 soft, x1.6 strong), roll 1.6 deg, linear decay | 0.06 s/step + 0.45 s rest | 3.6 x 5 + 27 |
| cursor press | scale 0.88 (-12%), ripple 2.4x | travel 0.48 s | 28.8 |
| dock / indicator stretch | dock overshoot 1.14, indicator scaleX 1.18 | glide 0.32 s | 19.2 |
| springs (section 3) | 0.4% to 12.6% overshoot | see settle frames | - |

Rule of thumb: overshoot 2-10% of the move (above ~15% reads cartoonish); the settle takes 1.3-1.7x the onset (onset to peak is 0.1-0.25 s), and 2-2.6x only for hero pops (measured on 33 real clips, see `references/measured/`); anticipation is about one third of the action time (UI: ~10% of the move over 3-6 frames). For wind-ups, overshoot and follow-through on shapes use `anticipate` / `launch` / `followThrough` from `src/core/physics.ts` (`docs/CHARACTER_ANIMATION.md`); do not hand-fake them.

In this repo: `pop` and `words` carry the overshoot; `punch` is the 7+12 frame beat emphasis; `shake` is the only multi-step recipe.

## 6. Space

Speed in px/frame = distance / frames. Eased speed peaks at `peak x average` (section 2). Measured on 33 real clips (`references/measured/`, 1080p, 60 fps): comfortable PEAK speed is <= ~30 px/f; impacts may reach <= ~80 px/f but only for <= 3 frames; sustained motion is 5-15 px/f; camera moves are 2-6 px/f. Above that an unblurred object jumps whole body-widths between frames and strobes.

| move | px | frames | avg px/f | peak px/f | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `rise` normal | 28 | 27 | 1.0 | 7.2 | comfortable |
| `rise` strong | 56 | 27 | 2.1 | 14.4 | comfortable |
| `words` drop | 42 | 22.8 | 1.8 | 8.5 | comfortable |
| `sink` exit | 48 | 19.2 | 2.5 | 7.5 | comfortable |
| logo-sting `ring-wrap` x | 193 | 39 | 4.9 | 34.3 | impact-only (<= 3 f) |
| light leak (distance token, at `camera` duration) | 1920 | 39 | 49.2 | 340.9 | needs blur / longer / gentler ease |
| BAD: 1500 px in 10 frames, expo.out | 1500 | 10 | 150.0 | 1038.8 | needs blur / longer / gentler ease |

Distance budget: with `expoOut` (peak 6.9x) over `camera` (39 f), peak stays <= 30 px/f only up to 169 px (<= 80 px/f, impacts only, up to 451 px). Beyond that add blur (`wordBlur` 8, `exitBlur` 12, `surfaceBlur` 14 px), lengthen the duration, or use `power3.out` (4.0x).

Arcs vs lines: living things and thrown objects travel in arcs (x and y on *different* eases or durations); a straight line with one ease reads mechanical, fine for UI. Transform-origin: scale/rotate pivot about it, a point `r` px from the origin moves `(s-1) x r`: at scale 1.1 a point 500 px away shifts 50 px. `wipe` pivots on the left edge, `wipeOut` on the right.

Parallax: apparent speed is proportional to 1/depth. With `multiplaneParallax` ratios near 1.5 / mid 1 / far 0.45 and a base shift of 100 px: near moves 150 px, mid 100, far 45 px; implied depth (mid = 1) near 0.67, far 2.22. Over `camera` (39 f): 3.85 / 2.56 / 1.15 px/f.

Camera scale vs distance: pinhole model, apparent size = 1/distance, so scale `s` means the camera moved `1 - 1/s` of the way.

In this repo: `camera(tl, sel, 'pushIn'|'pullOut'|'settle', at, { intensity })`; `SCALE_CONTRAST` for big leaps; never tween top/left (T005), use x/y.

## 7. Composition & rhythm

One focal point at a time: only one element moves hard while the others are still or drifting. Vary beat lengths (sigma of lengths > 14 f is the repo bar); a drumbeat of equal beats feels like a slideshow. Beat plans from the golden examples (length = end - start + 1):

| example | format | beats (frames) | sigma (f) | final hold |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `kinetic-type` | 9:16, 6 s (360 f) | hook 96 f (1.6 s), turn 126 f (2.1 s), sign 139 f (2.32 s) | 18.0 | 72 f (f288-f360) inside a 139 f last beat |
| `product-ui` | 16:9, 8 s (480 f) | open 120 f (2 s), act 132 f (2.2 s), finale 229 f (3.82 s) | 48.8 | 72 f (f408-f480) inside a 229 f last beat |
| `logo-sting` | 16:9, 4 s (240 f) | form 90 f (1.5 s), resolve 151 f (2.52 s) | 30.5 | 72 f (f168-f240) inside a 151 f last beat |

Hold math: `holdFinal(tl, at, 1.2)` = 72 f of stillness; only `camera:drift` may move inside it. Last tween must end before `hold:start`, so `hold:start = video end - 1.2 s`. Micro-beat pulse: something changes (focus, camera, depth) every 60-90 f (1.0-1.5 s).

In this repo: `beat(tl, id, at)` at the spec start frame, T001 +-2 f; every beat needs a tween starting inside it (T002).

## 9. Common mistakes -> fix

| symptom | why (math / perception) | fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entrance uses an `.in` ease | speed peaks at the end (3.0x avg for `quickIn`) then stops dead: reads as a slam | `enter(...)` presets use `.out`; `.in` is for `exit` |
| Everything starts on the same frame | no hierarchy, eye has no order; one big blob of change | `enter(.., 'stagger'|'words', at, { stagger })`, 3.3 f apart |
| Equal-length beats | sigma < 14 f = metronome; viewer predicts every cut and tunes out | vary beats 1-2.5 s (see section 7 plans) |
| 2 s opacity fade between scenes | 120 f of near-invisible change; slide-deck dissolve (banned by storyboard law 1) | `transition(tl, 'maskWipe'|'morph'|'zoomPunch'|..)`, 0.6-0.8 s |
| Scale 0 to 1 with `linear` | constant speed, no settle: looks like a progress bar; first frames are tiny and slow to notice | `enter(.., 'pop')`: overshoot 1.06 then settle |
| Moving 1500 px in 10 frames | 150 px/f average, 1039 px/f peak with expo.out: strobing, no tracking | distance budget in section 6; blur, longer duration, or `camera:pushIn` |
| Text visible for 0.6 s | 36 f; repo floor for 3 words is 1.7 s (102 f) | hold via `calculateRequiredReadingTime`; shorten copy |
| Three transitions back to back | ~0.6-0.81 s each: >2 s with no stable frame; T007 warns on overlap | one transition per beat boundary, then an `enter` and a still moment |
| Entrance with `inOut` | power2.inOut is 0.06 at 25% time: first quarter of the clip barely moves | `.out` for arriving things |
| Final CTA animates until the last frame | needs 72 f of stillness (storyboard law 2, T003) | `holdFinal(tl, end - 1.2, 1.2)`; only `camera:drift` inside |
| Tweening width/height/top/left | triggers layout reflow every frame, not GPU, jitters | x, y, scale, scaleX with `transformOrigin` (T005) |
| Bouncy spring on a heavy panel | bouncy: 13% overshoot, 63 f settle; heavy things should not wobble | `SPRING.rigid` / `smooth` (zeta >= 1) |
| Camera push 2.85x with no blur | 65% closer: every pixel moves fast near the edges | `pushIn` strong (1.18) or add `blurIn`/`dive` transition |
| Dead beat: nothing starts inside | the frame is static for the full beat | add `emphasize`/`camera` preset in the beat (T002) |

In this repo: if unsure, run `pnpm check --project <slug>`; T/A/V2 codes in `RULES.md` section 2 map to the rows above.
