# Direction - Margin Manifesto

## Message (one-sentence goal)
Margin is the notes app for people who believe short writing is better thinking.

## Audience
Writers and product people scrolling a vertical feed; sound off, so type carries everything.

## Look
- Palette: theme "editorial-warm"; a dark ink panel (textPrimary) takes over in the middle
- Type: ultra-heavy sans, one word per line, 150-190px; closing tagline 110px

## Beats
| id | seconds | what happens | presets |
|---|---|---|---|
| hook | 1.60 | "Most writing is too long." drops in word by word, last word punches | words, punch, drift |
| turn | 2.10 | diagonal wipe to ink panel; "Cut it. Then cut it again." staggers in | maskWipe, stagger, punch, pushIn |
| sign | 2.30 | mantra sinks away; mark pops, tagline words land, 1.2s hold | sink, pop, words |
