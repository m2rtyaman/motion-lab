# Direction — YOUTUBE PLAYER

## One-Sentence Core Goal
Transform YouTube's global brand identity and authentic interface anatomy (Search Bar, Watch Page, Subscribe Button, Recommended Feed) into a living motion graphic through a full-bleed Theatre Mode scale leap.

## Paradigm
hybrid-orchestrated

## Three-Input Separation of Concerns (The 3-Input Rule)
> Reference: references/MASTER_STORYBOARD_RULES.md & src/projects/youtube-player/choreography-blueprint.md
- **Script (What & Why):** Content discovery on the world's leading video platform: starting from search, transitioning into a rich watch page, and exploding into a full-bleed cinematic viewing experience.
- **Storyboard (Visual Plan - How It Looks):** 
  - Beat 1 (0–110f): Pure white (#FFFFFF / #F9F9F9) YouTube canvas. Official YouTube Header at the top (Red logo, pill search bar, magnifying glass, microphone, user avatar). Typewriter input inside search pill, curved cursor selects top result.
  - Beat 2 (111–270f): Authentic YouTube Watch Page: 16:9 video player, title, verified channel badge ("Motion Lab"), "2.4M subscribers", iconic black "Subscribe" pill, Like/Share action pills, and 3-card recommended feed. Cursor clicks "Theatre Mode" icon.
  - Beat 3 (271–420f): Theatre Mode Explosion: Video player executes a dramatic 20x scale leap (Peak Velocity Container Takeover) taking over the full viewport! Red scrubber bar sweeps across, kinetic typography ("EXPERIENCE BOLD MOTION") punches through.
  - Beat 4 (421–600f): Monumental Brand Resolve: The full-bleed player seamlessly morphs into YouTube's iconic emblem via continuous kinetic handoff (`TheHeroActor`). Official YouTube Red Play mark + YouTube logotype + "Watch Now" CTA holds static for 1.8s (110 frames @ 60 FPS).
- **Motion Reference (How It Moves):**
  - Continuous Hero Actor: Search bar $\rightarrow$ video player $\rightarrow$ full-bleed theatre mode $\rightarrow$ red brand emblem.
  - Anticipatory stillness (300ms) before snappy click impulses.
  - Zero muddy crossfades: Peak velocity container morphing and full-screen scale leap.

## Three-Layer Depth Architecture
- **Layer 1 (Ground Base):** Crisp YouTube white (`#FFFFFF` / `#F9F9F9`), subtle material drop shadows (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`), diffused ambient red floor glow.
- **Layer 2 (Secondary Depth & Ghosting):** YouTube watch stage components, video cards, recommendation feed column, and red scrubber progress trail.
- **Layer 3 (Foreground Hero Actor):** Razor-sharp typography, official YouTube Play SVG emblem (`path d="M23.498 6.186..."`), black "Subscribe" pill, and kinetic typography burst.

## TheHeroActor Lifecycle
`[Search Pill & Header]` ➔ `[Authentic 16:9 Video Watch Page]` ➔ `[Full-Bleed Theatre Mode Takeover]` ➔ `[Monumental YouTube Logo Resolve (1.8s Stillness Hold)]`

## Typography & Palette
- **Palette:** Clean White (`#FFFFFF`), Light Canvas (`#F9F9F9`), Border Gray (`#E5E5E5`), YouTube Red (`#FF0000`), Pure Black (`#0F0F0F`), Secondary Gray (`#606060`).
- **Typography:** Syne & Inter Display, min 18px broadcast standard.
