# Direction - Tallybook Month-End

## Message (one-sentence goal)
Tallybook reconciles a whole month of transactions in a single click.

## Audience
Small-studio owners and bookkeepers watching a 16:9 landing-page hero; sound off.

## Look
- Palette: theme "light" (slate neutrals, one royal-blue accent); finale canvas is the accent color
- Type: geometric sans; headline 56px, UI text from the shells, finale headline 96px

## Beats
| id | seconds | what happens | presets |
|---|---|---|---|
| open | 2.00 | BrowserFrame blurs in; headline words, header row and one large chart arrive | blurIn, words, rise, drift |
| act | 2.20 | cursor glides to "Reconcile all", clicks (ripple + punch), camera pushes in | punch, pushIn |
| finale | 3.80 | iris wipe to the brand canvas; mark pops, words land, CTA pulses, 1.2s hold | maskWipe, pop, words, rise, punch |
