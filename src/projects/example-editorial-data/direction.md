# Direction - Ardmore Cycling Data Story

## Message (one-sentence goal)
Weekday bike trips downtown are 3.4 times higher than in 2019, and the protected lanes are why.

## Audience
Readers of an editorial newsletter or a city-news feed, 16:9, sound off; they should remember one number.

## Look
- Theme "editorial-warm": paper canvas, one burnt-orange accent used for the numeral, the 2025 bar and the takeaway rule
- Type: 400px numeral (scales to 200px header), 72px takeaway, 48px label, 28px chart values; faint grid backdrop
- Chart: 7 neutral bars, the story bar in the accent, one dashed annotation at 2022

## Beats
| id | seconds | what happens | presets |
|---|---|---|---|
| stat | 2.20 | numeral blurs in, label rises | blurIn, rise, drift |
| chart | 1.60 | numeral and label move to the header, baseline and bars build | expoInOut move, wipe, stagger, scaleY |
| lanes | 1.80 | years before 2022 dim, annotation names the cause | opacity, rise |
| takeaway | 2.40 | last bar punches, takeaway words land, source rises, 1.2s hold | punch, wipe, words, rise |
