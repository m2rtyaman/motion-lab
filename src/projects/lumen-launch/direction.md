# Direction - LUMEN LAUNCH

## Paradigm & Theme
- **Paradigm:** `editorial-ui` (16:9, 14 seconds, 840 frames @ 60 FPS)
- **Theme:** `light` (Pristine Light SaaS Theme: slate-50 canvas `#f8fafc`, pure white glass cards `#ffffff`, crisp 1px borders `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`, royal blue accents `#2563eb`, emerald status `#10b981`, deep slate-900 typography `#0f172a`).

## Visual Concept & Narrative
Bu videonun tek cümlelik hedefi şu: Yüksek tempolu teknoloji ekiplerine, takvim sürtünmesini ve toplantı çatışmalarını otonom çözen ve haftalık 18.4 saat odaklanma zamanı kazandıran aydınlık, pürüzsüz Lumen işletim sistemini kanıtlamaktır.

Lumen is the autonomous scheduling intelligence for high-velocity teams. This launch film rejects the dark-glass sci-fi cliché and delivers a luminous, crisp, tactile product demo that feels like an Apple Keynote meets Linear.

1. **Beat 1: Omni-Command Bar (0.0s - 3.32s, f0-199)**
   A minimalist floating command bar surfaces in the viewport. Smart typing simulates instant intent capture ("⌘K Schedule Core Strategy Sync"). Intelligent participant pills snap in.
2. **Transition 1: Container Morph & Whip-Pan (3.17s, f190)**
   The command bar does not unmount. Following the Single Container Principle, its boundaries expand with organic spring physics to become the weekly schedule viewport, while a directional velocity streak sweeps the workspace into position.
3. **Beat 2: Autonomous Conflict Resolution (3.33s - 7.48s, f200-449)**
   A crisp 5-day time grid (Mon-Fri) with color-coded event cards. An incoming conflicting block slides in with magnetic velocity. An amber alert badge ("Collision Detected") immediately triggers Lumen's auto-resolver: the block glides seamlessly to 15:30, resolving the conflict into an emerald success pill ("Optimal Focus Preserved").
4. **Transition 2: Specular Blade Mask Sweep (7.33s, f440)**
   A diagonal specular light blade wipes across the screen from top-right to bottom-left. The calendar retreats into depth as the telemetry dashboard slides in with matched velocity.
5. **Beat 3: Autonomous Time Telemetry (7.50s - 10.65s, f450-639)**
   Real-time analytics proving time saved. Animated SVG bezier sparklines draw dynamically, a live counting metric reaches `+18.4 hrs/week`, and team synchronization rings pulse with precision.
6. **Transition 3: Differential Zoom Punch (10.50s, f630)**
   The telemetry dashboard hyper-dives toward the camera, blurring out, while an expanding aperture ring centers the viewer's gaze.
7. **Beat 4: Monolithic Brand Payoff & CTA (10.67s - 14.0s, f640-840)**
   A 300ms anticipatory stillness before the final resolve. A monolithic dual-prism Lumen brand emblem locks into place, flanked by crystal-clear typography ("RECLAIM YOUR FOCUS") and an interactive pill CTA with specular highlight.

## Camera Discipline
- Steady focus holds during all content interactions and text reading.
- Decisive, motivated camera pushes locked to transition boundaries.
- Zero random wobble or artificial drifting.
