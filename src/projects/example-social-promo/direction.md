# Direction - Brewmark Social Promo

## Message (one-sentence goal)
Brewmark lets you order the usual before you reach the counter, so you skip the line.

## Audience
Commuters scrolling a 9:16 feed with the sound off; they decide in the first second.

## Look
- Theme "vibrant-brand": navy canvas, slate card, one orange accent for every action (order button, CTA); the toast is the only light surface
- Type: 120px display headline, 56px card title and CTA, 32px support, 24px labels; every UI text is 24px or larger
- Depth: rings backdrop, e3 card shadow, the character on a thin ground line
- Layout: headline in the top third; a large 680px card and toast right of centre in the middle; the character (scaled 1.2, 790px tall) stands left on a ground line at y 1420, its head level with the card top; the 56px CTA pill sits in the bottom band (y 1500-1630) as the final focal

## Beats
| id | seconds | what happens | presets |
|---|---|---|---|
| hook | 2.50 | headline words drop in, the character walks in from the left edge | words, wipe, rigWalk, drift |
| card | 1.67 | order card blurs in, the character looks at it | blurIn, lookAt |
| tap | 1.83 | cursor taps the orange button, camera leans in, toast pops, the character reacts | punch, pushIn, pop, react |
| cta | 2.00 | pill rises, one punch, 1.2s hold | rise, punch |
