# Direction - Orbit Logo Sting

## Message (one-sentence goal)
Orbit is a calm, precise scheduling tool; the logo should feel like something settling into place.

## Audience
Viewers of a product video outro or intro, 16:9, any sound.

## Look
- Palette: theme "dark"; one accent (accentPrimary) used only on the ring and the dot
- Type: heavy geometric sans wordmark 120px, tagline 36px muted

## Beats
| id | seconds | what happens | presets |
|---|---|---|---|
| form | 1.50 | ring wipes in over a faint grid; a small dot pops at its edge | wipe, pop, drift |
| resolve | 2.50 | ring settles, wordmark blurs in sharp, tagline rises, 1.2s hold | settle, blurIn, rise |
