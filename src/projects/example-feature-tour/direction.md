# Direction - Foldline Feature Tour

## Message (one-sentence goal)
Foldline plans a day in three clicks: add a task, pick a date, share the list.

## Audience
Visitors of a product landing page, 16:9, sound off; they should see each click cause its result.

## Look
- Theme "dark": one blue accent (number badges, due chip, CTA), inverted white surfaces for the quick-add bar, menu and toast
- One continuous app window (1440 x 800, text 24-28px); the camera (`.cam`, scale 1.6) pushes to each feature and pulls back for the recap
- Captions live in a reserved 120px band under the 960px stage (never over UI); the closing card dims the window and centres headline + CTA

## Beats
| id | seconds | what happens | presets |
|---|---|---|---|
| open | 1.80 | window blurs in under the headline | blurIn, words, drift |
| capture | 2.50 | camera pushes in, cursor clicks Add a task, new row slides in | camera move, punch, rise |
| schedule | 2.33 | cursor opens the due chip menu and picks Friday, the chip fills | pop, punch, sink |
| share | 2.04 | cursor clicks Share, avatars and toast appear, camera pulls back | pop, sink, camera move |
| outro | 1.83 | closing line and CTA land, window recap holds 1.2s | words, rise |
