# Direction - Tidemark Collections

## Message (one-sentence goal)
Tidemark sorts your saved links into collections with one click, so the library tidies itself.

## Audience
Landing-page and launch-post viewers, 16:9, sound optional.

## Look
- Palette: theme "light"; accentPrimary for the button, collection outlines and the finale canvas
- Type: 72px headline, 30px UI rows, nothing under 24px; the UI lives inside a browser frame (no floating cards)

## Beats
| id | seconds | what happens | presets |
|---|---|---|---|
| open | 2.00 | frame blurs in, headline words drop, five saved links queue up | blurIn, words, stagger, drift |
| act | 2.40 | cursor clicks the sort button, collections stagger in, camera leans in, iris wipe starts | punch, pushIn, stagger, maskWipe |
| finale | 3.60 | brand mark pops, tagline words, CTA rises and pulses, 1.2s hold | pop, words, rise, punch |
