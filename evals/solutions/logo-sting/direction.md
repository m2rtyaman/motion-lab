# Direction - Halcyon Sting

## Message (one-sentence goal)
Halcyon is calm software; the logo should feel like water settling under a low sun.

## Audience
Viewers of a product video intro or outro, 16:9, any sound.

## Look
- Palette: theme "dark"; accentPrimary on the waves, accentSecondary on the sun only
- Type: black wordmark 112px, tagline 40px muted

## Beats
| id | seconds | what happens | presets |
|---|---|---|---|
| form | 1.30 | three waves rise in sequence, the sun pops above them | stagger, pop, drift |
| resolve | 2.70 | mark slides left (sine.inOut), wordmark blurs in sharp, tagline rises, 1.2s hold | blurIn, rise |
