# Direction - How We Work

## Message (one-sentence goal)
Five steps, delivered in phrases rather than a metronome, so "Animate" is the one the viewer remembers.

## Audience
Agency intro or about-page video, 16:9, sound optional.

## Look
- Palette: theme "dark"; accentPrimary on the rule and the hero step, accentSecondary on the closing line
- Type: 150px black title, 64px step names, 28px muted notes; steps sit in a 1px hairline matrix

## Beats
| id | seconds | what happens | presets |
|---|---|---|---|
| title | 1.00 | rule wipes in, "How we work" drops word by word | wipe, words, drift |
| steps | 2.00 | onsets at 1.10, 1.22, 1.34 (burst), 2.00 (hero, strong + punch), 2.50 | rise x5, punch |
| close | 2.00 | list leans in, closing line rises, 1.2s hold | pushIn, rise |
