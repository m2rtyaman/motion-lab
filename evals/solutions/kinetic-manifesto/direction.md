# Direction - Signal Manifesto

## Message (one-sentence goal)
Three short lines, each one replacing the last, so the viewer remembers "say it once".

## Audience
Vertical social feed, 9:16, sound optional.

## Look
- Palette: theme "dark"; the last word of each line takes the accent colour
- Type: 176px black uppercase, two words stacked per line, inside the 250/300 safe zone

## Beats
| id | seconds | what happens | presets |
|---|---|---|---|
| less | 1.50 | bar wipes in, "Less noise." drops in word by word, last word punches | wipe, words, punch, drift |
| more | 1.70 | line one sinks, "More signal." drops in under a slow push-in | sink, words, pushIn, punch |
| once | 2.80 | line two sinks, camera settles, "Say it once." lands, dot pops, 1.2s hold | sink, settle, words, pop |
