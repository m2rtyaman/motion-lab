# Direction - Harbor Line Riders

## Message (one-sentence goal)
One number, 48,200, counting up fast and then standing still long enough to be remembered.

## Audience
Vertical social feed, 9:16, sound optional.

## Look
- Palette: theme "dark"; accentPrimary on the label, accentSecondary on the underline
- Type: 240px black tabular numerals (no jitter while counting), 56px label, 40px mono kicker; centred inside the 250/300 safe zone

## Beats
| id | seconds | what happens | presets |
|---|---|---|---|
| build | 1.00 | rule wipes in, kicker rises | wipe, rise, drift |
| count | 1.70 | number pops in, counts 0 to 48,200 in 1.4 s (cubic out, frame-driven), push-in, punch | pop, pushIn, punch |
| land | 2.30 | label rises, underline wipes, 1.2s hold | rise, wipe |
